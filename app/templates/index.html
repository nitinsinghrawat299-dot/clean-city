Built for the Garhwal Himalayas

Rooted in Pauri Garhwal

SPR started close to home — in the hill towns and valleys of Pauri Garhwal district, Uttarakhand. Spread across the lower Himalayas and the Gangetic plain, the district's rugged terrain and scattered villages make traditional waste-reporting slow. A geo-pinned photo report closes that gap instantly.

Most residents here speak Garhwali as a first language, and the district's forests, temples, and hill stations draw visitors year-round — which is exactly why keeping shared spaces clean matters to both locals and travelers.
